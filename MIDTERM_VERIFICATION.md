# A. GROUP VERIFICATION

### 1. What is the biggest issue your team still needs to solve before Week 6?
*(Choose one issue only. It may be about financial logic, data, assumptions, scoring, MVP scope, user flow, interface, or technical feasibility.)*

**Answer:**  
Our biggest issue is clearly differentiating **Free Fall 2.0 (K63)** from the previous iteration, **Free Fall 1 (K62)** (deployed at [financialcrisisftu.netlify.app](https://financialcrisisftu.netlify.app/)). Although both projects are financial learning games set within market-crisis scenarios requiring players to make investment choices across six phases, K63 must establish a fundamentally distinct core gameplay experience, financial engine, and learning objective.

Free Fall 2.0 focuses specifically on **leverage blindness and the destructive consequences of margin trading**. Its core mechanics involve **Initial Capital, Margin Tiers (1× to 4×), Margin Debt, Net Equity, Effective Leverage, Margin Ratio, Margin Calls, Involuntary Forced Liquidation (at a 20% maintenance margin floor), and Property Target Progress (3× Small House, 20× Normal House, 100× ToLam Villa)**. Rather than engaging in a turn-based information-gathering puzzle using a "Research Budget" as in K62, the player directly experiences how leverage amplifies both paper gains and sudden solvency pressure during an active market crash.

Therefore, our main issue before Week 6 is ensuring that these **margin-trading, debt obligations, and forced-liquidation mechanics remain central to K63**, rather than allowing the MVP to appear too similar to K62's broader, debt-free macroeconomic decision-making structure.


### 2. Why is this issue important?
*(Explain what part of your core product depends on it and what may go wrong if the issue is not solved.)*

**Answer:**  
This issue is critical because the core identity and academic value of Free Fall 2.0 depend entirely on its distinct learning objective and quantitative financial mechanics. If the distinction between K63 and K62 is not rigorously maintained, our product risks being perceived merely as a cosmetic variation of the 2008 GFC simulation rather than an original, mathematically driven margin simulation of the mid-2026 Korean stock market crisis.

For K63, the user must viscerally experience the direct mathematical relationship connecting **Leverage → Market Price Drops → Shrinking Equity → Margin Calls → Involuntary Broker Liquidation**. In K62, players can underperform and receive a lower decision score, but they never experience solvency ruin or broker-enforced fire-sales. If these margin-trading elements are not placed front and center in K63's gameplay, the project will lose its intended focus on **leverage blindness** and fail to teach retail investors and students why borrowed money turns manageable market downturns into total capital wipeouts.


### 3. What has your team done about this issue so far?
*(Briefly describe what you have already done to understand or solve it—for example: checked a source, tested a calculation, compared options, built a sample case, tried a prototype, changed the design, or simplified the scope.)*

**Answer:**  
Our team has audited the K63 architecture against K62 to establish structural and mechanical divergence across all four project pillars:
1. **Financial Logic & Solvency Engine:** Shifted entirely away from K62's un-leveraged asset allocation to a full margin accounting engine in `src/simulation_engine.py`. The engine continuously calculates:

   $$\text{Margin Ratio} = \frac{\text{Equity}}{\text{Stock Portfolio Value}} = \frac{\text{Cash} + \text{Total Portfolio Value} - \text{Margin Debt}}{\sum (\text{Shares}_i \times P_i)}$$

   with an automated trigger executing a complete fire-sale plus a 5% liquidation penalty fee when the ratio breaches the **20% maintenance margin threshold**.
2. **Deterministic High-Frequency Market Dataset:** Replaced K62's turn-based news releases with `market scenario/market_scenario.csv`, comprising a **1,800-second (ticks) × 50-asset dataset** across six phases, calibrated to test achievable wealth ceilings under Cash 1×, 2×, 3×, and 4× strategies (`best_case_portfolio_summary.csv`).
3. **Core Player Actions & Hedging Flow:** Centered player inputs on active execution (**Buy, Sell, Hold**) with **Bank Savings (4.75% fixed yield)** acting as a safe-haven de-risking allocation, regulated by a T+0.5 settlement delay holding pen.
4. **Target Incentive Structure:** Tied leverage decisions directly to **Property Target Progress** (Small House 3×, Normal House 20×, ToLam Villa 100×), creating an authentic behavioral driver that induces the greed and FOMO necessary for leverage blindness to take root.
5. **Executable Verification:** Validated this logic via 4 automated unit tests (`tests/test_simulation_engine.py`) and an interactive terminal prototype (`interactive_demo.py`).


### 4. What will your team do next about this issue?
*(State the next concrete action before or during Week 6—for example: test more cases, replace a data source, change a rule, simplify the MVP, remove a feature, build a small prototype, or validate a calculation.)*

**Answer:**  
Before and during Week 6, our team will refine the **MVP user flow and trading-terminal interface** to guarantee that the margin-trading mechanism remains the central, unambiguous gameplay element.

Specifically, we will test and streamline the core interaction sequence:

**Selecting Initial Capital and a Margin Tier (1×–4×) → Placing Trades → Experiencing 1,800-Tick Market Movements → Observing Real-Time Changes in Equity and Margin Ratio → Responding to Margin Pressure (De-risking into Bank Savings) → Surviving or Experiencing Involuntary Forced Liquidation**

To reinforce this distinction, we will:
1. **Streamline Scope:** Remove or avoid complex peripheral features (such as order-book depth queues, cryptocurrency pairs, or multi-tab research files) that dilute focus or mimic K62's information-puzzle format.
2. **Dynamic Margin Health Visualization:** Bind backend margin ratios directly to the UI's dynamic **Margin Health Gauge** (`web/index.html`), ensuring flashing visual warning states at 25% and immediate liquidation alerts below 20%.
3. **Keep the Educational Core Uncompromised:** Ensure every player exit state directly diagnoses whether the user succumbed to **leverage blindness** or successfully practiced prudent solvency management.
